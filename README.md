**Cold Healing** is an immersive medical expansion mod designed for hardcore survival environments. Inspired by the tactical depth of realistic survival titles, it introduces a refined vision of medical functionality to Minecraft, replacing "arcade-style" instant healing with a more deliberate, strategic system.

The primary focus is **functionality** and **compatibility**. Thanks to its powerful configuration system, Cold Healing is designed to integrate seamlessly into any complex modpack.

### 🧩 Compatibility
Built with the modern modding ecosystem in mind, **Cold Healing** features native, out-of-the-box compatibility with:
*   [Legendary Survival Overhaul (LSO)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/legendary-survival-overhaul)
*   [Curios API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios)

---

### 📦 Main Items
*Each item has unique mechanics and scales its functionality when used alongside LSO.*

*   **IFAK**: A rapid-use, low-durability kit designed to quickly stop negative effects.
  
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Restores a small amount of health to the most damaged limb (above 0 HP).
      
*   **AFAK**: Similar to the IFAK but more durable and slightly slower. When it reaches its last use, it enters a "second stage" with a longer use time that heals significantly more.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* The second stage heals all body parts (above 0 HP).
*   **CMS**: A precise surgery kit with low health regeneration.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Capable of restoring body parts that have reached 0 HP (surgical repair).
*   **CAR**: A durable, high-tier kit with two modes: effect removal or high health regeneration.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Both modes can heal body parts that are above 0 HP.
*   **SPLINT**: A quick, single-use item to clear mobility effects like Slowness. Cannot be used if certain "incompatible" effects are active.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Repairs broken arms or legs (0 HP).
*   **IBUPROFEN BOTTLE**: Used to clear effects. It shares the same restriction logic as the Splint regarding incompatible effects.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Grants the **Painkiller** effect and manages **Painkiller Addiction**.
*   **P-04 STABILIZER STIM**: Quick-use stimulant for effect removal and instant health boost.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Heals any upper body part (above 0 HP).
*   **ADRENO NEURAL S3 STIM**: Focuses on agility and speed instead of raw health boost.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Heals any lower body part (above 0 HP).
*   **HEMATOX-G STIM**: Provides a long-duration health regeneration effect.
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Gradually heals all body parts (above 0 HP).
*   **S9 N-BLOCK STIM**: Specifically targets nervous system effects (e.g., Blindness).
    *   *LSO Compatibility:* Functions like Ibuprofen but can be used at any time without restrictions.

### 🧪 Miscellaneous
*   **MELATONIN BOTTLE**: Allows the player to sleep anywhere, at any time, with varied sleep durations.
  
*   **STIM POUCH**: A specialized container that can store up to 6 stimulants.
    *   *Curios Compatibility:* When equipped in a Curios slot, stimulants inside will be **used automatically** when the player hits specific health or effect thresholds.
